# Target roles: keep / drop rules

## Titles to search (all five)

1. Trading Analyst (Loan Closing / Loan Settlement)
2. Private Credit Analyst
3. Private Credit Associate
4. Leveraged Finance / Loan Execution
5. Transaction Management
6. Credit Risk Strategy
7. Asset Management Operations

(These map onto the five resume variants; the loan-closing trading-analyst and
private-credit-analyst titles are the primary two, the rest are adjacent targets.)

Match generously on title synonyms: "Loan Closer", "Loan Admin", "Loan Servicing",
"Syndicated Loan", "Par Loan", "Agency", "Credit Analyst", "Credit Associate",
"Loan Execution", "Trade Support (Loans)", "Settlements Analyst".

## Seniority

- **Keep:** Analyst, Senior Analyst, Associate, Senior Associate, mid-level.
- **Drop (hard):** anything VP, Vice President, Director, ED, MD, Head of, or Lead.
- **Drop:** graduate/intern/placement roles (below Desiree's level).

## Location

- **Keep:** London (any London borough), plus hybrid or remote roles that are
  explicitly UK-based and reachable from London.
- **Drop:** roles requiring a non-UK base, or UK roles tied to a non-London office
  with no London option.
- Work authorization: UK citizen, **no sponsorship required**, so do not drop a role
  for visa reasons.

## Role-types to exclude (hard)

- VP and above (see seniority).
- Sales, business development, relationship management, origination-only front office.
- Compliance, internal audit, regulatory reporting, KYC/AML.
- Pure software engineering, quant developer, data engineering, DevOps.
- Unrelated back-office (HR, procurement, facilities) not tied to loans or credit.

## Freshness

- Keep only roles **posted or updated in the last 14 days**.

## Dedup and cooldown

- Drop anything already keyed in `seen-roles.json`.
- Hard-block any company applied to in the **last 14 days** (read `applications.md`).
- Soft-flag any company applied to **14 to 90 days** ago.
